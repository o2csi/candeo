Games

Your health on the number row, your clip on the function keys, the keyboard white when a flashbang goes off and red while the bomb ticks. Counter-Strike 2 tells Candeo what happens in your game, and your keyboard shows it.

Connect Counter-Strike 2

  1. In Candeo, Settings › Games, click Connect next to Counter-Strike 2.
  2. Start the game — or restart it if it was running: it reads the connection when it starts.
  3. In the gallery, apply the Counter-Strike 2 effect to your keyboard.

To try it without a real match: Play › Practice › With bots. With the developer console on and sv_cheats 1, give weapon_flashbang or, playing terrorist, give weapon_c4 tries the flash and the bomb.

The Counter-Strike 2 effect

  • Your health fills the number row, from green to red as it drops.
  • The clip of the weapon in hand fills the function row.
  • A flashbang turns the keyboard white, as it turns your screen.
  • Fire turns it orange.
  • The planted bomb makes the whole keyboard pulse red.

Every colour is a setting, and so is the flash's strength. Before the game says anything, both bars glow faintly: waiting, not broken.

Make it your own

What the game says arrives as signals, so everything signals do works with it — a rule, a setting following a value, an effect of your own:

Signal Value
cs2.health cs2.armor0 to 100
cs2.ammothe clip of the weapon in hand, 0 to 100 %
cs2.flashed cs2.burning0 to 100
cs2.bombnone, planted, defused or exploded
cs2.phasefreezetime, live or over
When cs2.bomb is exploded · on the keyboard · show Lightning · for 3 seconds

Only your own values: while you spectate a teammate, theirs are not shown as yours. They last fifteen seconds and the game renews them every five, so quitting the game leaves nothing frozen on the keys.

How it works

Counter-Strike 2 has a feature of its own, Valve's game state integration: a small file in the game's folder names where to send its state, and the game sends it there as it changes. Connect writes that file, pointing at Candeo on your computer. Nothing reads the game's memory or its screen — it is the game telling what it chooses to tell, which is why it is safe with anti-cheat.

It goes to Candeo's local signals API, behind a token of its own, and stays in memory: nothing of your game reaches the disk or the log, and nothing leaves your computer. Disconnect removes the file.

Dota 2 has the same integration, and is next. The design is in docs/design/game-state-integration.md.
