Devices

Candeo drives lit devices that speak HID over USB. A device is listed here once its protocol has been surveyed on the hardware and verified by reading the writes back — the survey is linked from its row, with the firmware it was established against.

Adding one

A device, reduced to its essentials, is three things — and the first two are data rather than code:

  • An identity — the vendor and product ids, and which interface of the composite device carries the lighting.
  • A layout — the matrix, and which of its cells sit under a key. An effect is written against the layout, so it runs on any device that declares one.
  • Its reports — how a frame becomes bytes, and the checksum that makes the device accept them. This is the part that has to be surveyed, on the device, by someone who owns it.

Each fact carries where it came from: the firmware and the command it was read by, the date, whether it was seen on the bus or written and read back, and — for anything drawn rather than measured — that it was drawn. docs/design/device-sdk.md sets that out, and #34 is where a contribution becomes reviewable by someone without the hardware.
