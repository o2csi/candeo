One device, several effects

Where a device's lights sit in several places — the Alienware 17 R5's keyboard, lid and base, the ring and the logo of the m18 R1 — Devices can split it into regions. Each region then runs its own effect, with its own settings and its own way of following the sound or a signal: the keyboard dancing to the music while the lid shows the CPU's temperature.

Yours is not listed?

Candeo shows a lighting device it cannot drive yet as Detected, not supported yet, without opening it. Report opens a form already filled in with what Candeo saw of it and the computer's model — never a serial number — so that someone can write its definition.
